LAMA TOPUP DEMO
================
1. Open index.html in a browser, or upload all 3 files to Render/Netlify.
2. This is a frontend-only demo for Free Fire and eFootball.
3. Replace the QR placeholder in index.html with your real QR image.
4. Do not accept real payments until you add a secure backend, server-side payment verification, order database, and webhook handling.
5. Package prices are demo values; edit them in script.js.
